# Brag Plan: Progetto Olanda 2.0

## What is this app?
A dark-mode football analytics app, built by a group of friends, that predicts match statistics (corners, cards, shots, goals, xG…) league by league and tells you which fixtures are hot, which bets win, and how the market moved.

## The angle
Matchday broadcast opener for a friends' betting lab. The whole thing is treated like a Saturday-night football TV intro — gold trophy, stadium glow, big numbers — but it's a group of friends' side project. The seriousness of the model vs. the landing-page line "Select a league to begin male pisello..." is the joke.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black stadium dark. "GUT FEELING." slams in, gets a hard strike-through, and "THE MODEL." replaces it. VO: the group chat has a feeling; this has a model.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The gold "Select Your League" card: cursor hovers, gold edge runs round it, click → flag wipe.
- The real Ajax vs Excelsior match page: 9.7 predicted corners with both kits and crests, then the probability-by-line ladder.
- The real Ajax team page: stadium photo, kit, season stats, next match and corners focus.

## Outro / punchline
Logo + "Progetto Olanda 2.0" returns, then the Silkscreen credit line scrambles in: "Powered by NickyBoy, Ciusbe, Baggianis, Giagulosky."

## User flow worth showing
Pick league (gold picker → nation → league) → match prediction hero with total, uncertainty and split → fixtures → match page → probability ladder → click Ajax → team page (all real screenshots).

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: Saturday-night football broadcast opener for a friends' betting lab
- Interpretation: big type, gold/emerald light, slow confident reveals and dramatic wipes; the narration stays warm and a little wry so the credits land as the joke.

## Duration: 24.5s

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady and clean)
- Music treatment: fade in 0.5s, bed ~0.3, ducked to ~0.13 under voiceover, fade out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset (≈110 BPM). Strong cues in window: 8.74s, 13.11s, 17.47s, 18.56s. Target 8.74s near the prediction total landing and 17.47/18.56s near the outro logo. Beat grid ≈0.55s apart — feature cards snap to every other beat (≥1.1s apart) for readability.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS breathes the stadium glow behind the total and the logo halo. No visualizer bars.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: strike-through hit in hook, cursor click on league card, count-up ticks on the total, card sounds on the ladder and team page, bell on logo.
- Restraint rule: nothing may compete with the voice; no SFX over narration peaks louder than the voice.

Team page with badge, kit and Johan Cruijff ArenA photo; 4th of 18, 13 pts, 4·1·1, 19:7, form D W L W W. Camera drifts across the stadium, then lands on Next match (vs Excelsior, 9.7, 52%) + Corners focus on the 17.47s cue.

### Scene 5 — Credits outro — 5.2s
Logo + title; VO says "Progetto Olanda" in Italian as the title lands; Silkscreen credits scramble in (no highlight).
